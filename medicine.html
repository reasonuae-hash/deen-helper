Ajwa Dates | تمر العجوة

Mentioned in Sahih al-Bukhari 5445.

Dates supply carbohydrates, fiber, and potassium. They are food, not a substitute for medical care.

Honey | العسل

The Quran mentions honey in 16:69.

Honey has studied antimicrobial properties and some evidence for relieving acute cough in children over one year. Do not give honey to infants under one.

Black Seed | الحبة السوداء

Mentioned in Sahih al-Bukhari 5688.

Contains thymoquinone and other studied compounds. Human clinical evidence is limited for treating diseases.

Olive Oil | زيت الزيتون

Olive trees are mentioned in the Quran (24:35).

Olive oil is rich in monounsaturated fats and is associated with cardiovascular benefits when replacing saturated fat.
